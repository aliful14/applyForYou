FAQ

Honest answers to the real questions.

Do you guarantee I will get a job?

No. Nobody ethical can. What we offer is consistent, quality applications submitted every week — and the time back so you can focus on interviews. Results vary depending on your target role, location, and market conditions.

Who actually applies — me or you?

We do, on your behalf, with your written consent. We send you an authorization form before we submit the first application, so everything is transparent and on the record.

How do I pay?

Interac e‑Transfer. After we review your resume and you're ready to start, we email you the payment details. Setup is one‑time: $200 (Essentials), $300 (Balanced), or $350 (Full Support), depending on the tier you choose. Monthly is $50 on Essentials and $45 on Balanced. On Full Support, monthly is $35 for the first 2 months, then $45. Billing continues monthly until you ask us to stop.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, any time, for any reason — no penalty and no awkward call. Just reply to any email and say "pause" or "cancel."

How do you protect my data?

Your resume and personal details are only used to apply to jobs on your behalf. We access your dedicated job-search Gmail through Google's official Delegate Access feature — no passwords are ever shared or stored. When you cancel, your data is deleted within 30 days. See the privacy page for details.

What job portals do you cover?

LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, and most company career sites (including Workday, Greenhouse, and Lever portals). A few complex multi‑step portals may take longer, but we handle them manually when they matter for your target roles.

Do you write cover letters from scratch for each job?

Yes — every cover letter is tailored to the specific role and company. No copy‑paste. That's the whole point.

How many applications per week?

We aim for 20 or more per week, but we prioritize fit over volume. If fewer than 20 strong-fit roles are available in the market that week, we will not pad the count with irrelevant postings. Quality over quantity — every application is tailored and human-reviewed before it goes out.

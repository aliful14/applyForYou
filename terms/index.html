ApplyForYou is a job application assistance service based in Canada. Contact: info@applyforyou.org.

1. What ApplyForYou Does

ApplyForYou is a virtual administrative assistant and job application data-entry service. We search publicly advertised job postings that match your goals and submit applications on your behalf, with your written authorization. Depending on your plan, we also assist with resume preparation, cover letter templates, LinkedIn profile guidance, and interview preparation.

ApplyForYou provides administrative data-entry labor only. We fill out job application forms and submit them using the information and materials you provide to us. That is the full scope of the service.

ApplyForYou is NOT a recruiter, staffing agency, or employment agency under the Ontario Employment Standards Act or any other applicable law. Specifically:

  • We do not source unadvertised or hidden job opportunities;
  • We do not have relationships with employers or hiring managers;
  • We do not place, refer, or match candidates into roles;
  • We do not negotiate directly with employers on your behalf;
  • We do not charge employers any fee — we are paid by you, the job seeker, strictly for administrative time and document work.

This distinction is important. Under Ontario law, acting as a "recruiter" or "employment agency" requires a formal license from the Ministry of Labour. ApplyForYou is not and does not operate as a licensed recruiter. If you are looking for recruitment or headhunting services, ApplyForYou is not the right service for you.

If you do not meet these requirements, do not sign up for the service.

3. No Guarantee of Employment

ApplyForYou provides administrative data-entry labor only. The service does not guarantee interview callbacks, job placements, employer responses, or employment of any kind.

You are paying for the administrative time and work spent preparing and submitting applications on your behalf — not for securing interviews, offers, or any particular outcome. Hiring decisions are made entirely by employers and depend on many factors outside our control, including market conditions, employer preferences, competition from other candidates, and your qualifications.

What we offer is consistent, quality job applications submitted on your behalf every week, using the information and materials you provide.

ApplyForYou aims to submit 20 or more targeted applications per week but does not guarantee a specific weekly volume. The number of applications submitted will depend on how many roles genuinely match your qualifications, preferences, and work authorization in a given week. We will never apply to irrelevant roles just to meet a number. Quality over quantity is how we operate.

No verbal or written statement from ApplyForYou — including anything said in emails, calls, or meetings — should be interpreted as a guarantee of any employment outcome or specific application volume unless explicitly stated in writing and signed by both parties.

8. Payment Terms

(a) Setup fee

A one-time setup fee is required before any work begins. The amount depends on your selected plan:

  • Essentials: $200 CAD
  • Balanced: $300 CAD
  • Full Support: $350 CAD

This fee covers the preparation work done before job applications begin, including:

  • Tailoring and optimizing your resume(s) for target roles;
  • Drafting or reviewing cover letter templates;
  • Reviewing and optimizing your LinkedIn profile.

The setup fee is non-refundable once any of this preparation work has started or been completed, even if job applications have not yet been submitted. By paying the setup fee, you acknowledge that work on your resume, cover letter, and LinkedIn profile will begin promptly and cannot be undone once started.

(b) Monthly subscription

The monthly subscription covers the ongoing day-to-day work of applying to jobs on your behalf, including:

  • Searching for suitable job postings that match your profile;
  • Submitting job applications to employers on your behalf;
  • Sending you weekly application summaries and tracking logs;
  • Ongoing communication and updates related to your applications.

After the setup fee is paid, your first monthly subscription payment is due on or before the first (1st) day of your second month of service. Subsequent payments are due on or before the first (1st) day of each calendar month thereafter. The monthly amount depends on your plan:

  • Essentials: $50 CAD/month
  • Balanced: $45 CAD/month
  • Full Support: $35 CAD/month (first 2 months), then $45 CAD/month

(c) Late payment — pause

If your monthly payment is not received by the first of the month, ApplyForYou will pause new job applications on your behalf until payment is received. Preparation work and materials already created remain in place.

(d) Late payment — cancellation

If payment is not received within two (2) weeks of the due date, your subscription will be cancelled. ApplyForYou will stop all work, cease using any account access you have provided, and handle your data as described in the Privacy Policy.

(e) Refunds

The one-time setup fee is non-refundable once any preparation work has started or been completed. Preparation work includes — but is not limited to — resume tailoring, cover letter drafting, and LinkedIn profile optimization. This work begins promptly after payment is received, so the refund window is limited.

If you cancel before ApplyForYou has started any preparation work whatsoever, ApplyForYou may issue a full refund of the setup fee at its sole discretion. You must notify us by email immediately at info@applyforyou.org to request this.

Monthly fees are non-refundable for the current billing period once that period has started, except in the case of cancellation without cause by ApplyForYou (see Section 9(c)), where unused days are refunded on a pro-rated basis.

(f) Payment method

All payments are made by Interac e-Transfer. Payment details are provided after you complete the intake form and agree to start. No payment is requested before you have reviewed and agreed to the service.

10. Initial Term and Renewal

All ApplyForYou service plans operate on a two-month initial term beginning on the date the first application is submitted on your behalf.

(a) End-of-term review

At the end of the second month, ApplyForYou will contact you by email to review the engagement and confirm whether both parties wish to continue. This review is a standard part of every plan — not a sign that anything has gone wrong.

(b) Renewal

If both ApplyForYou and the client confirm they wish to continue, the service renews on a month-to-month basis at the standard monthly rate for the selected plan. No new setup fee is charged on renewal.

(c) Non-renewal

Either party may choose not to continue beyond the initial two-month term for any reason. No explanation is required and no penalty applies.

If ApplyForYou does not receive a response from the client within five (5) business days of the end-of-term review email, the service will not renew automatically and will end at the close of the second month.

(d) Effect of non-renewal

Upon non-renewal:

  • No further applications will be submitted;
  • Monthly billing stops at the end of the second month;
  • Any account access provided will be revoked;
  • Client data will be deleted within 30 days as described in the Privacy Policy.

The setup fee and fees for the two completed months are non-refundable.

13. Dedicated Job-Search Email

ApplyForYou requires clients to create a dedicated Gmail account used solely for job applications. This account should not be your personal Gmail — it is a separate account created exclusively for your job search.

Once the dedicated account is created, the client grants ApplyForYou delegate access to that Gmail account using Google's official Delegate Access feature (Settings → See all settings → Accounts → Grant access to your account). This allows ApplyForYou to read, manage, and respond to job-related emails on your behalf without ever knowing your password.

Benefits of this approach:

  • No password is shared with ApplyForYou at any point;
  • Access is granted and revoked entirely within your Google account settings — you are in full control at all times;
  • This is an official Google feature designed for delegated inbox management, with no violation of Google's Terms of Service;
  • Emails sent by ApplyForYou on your behalf are clearly labeled "sent by ApplyForYou" within Google's system.

ApplyForYou does not require access to your personal Gmail account. Your personal email password is never requested or stored.

Any delegate access you provide is governed by the Client Authorization Form you sign before the service begins. You may revoke delegate access at any time from your Google account settings.

APPLYFORYOU — THREE PRICING TIERS (WORKING SPEC)
================================================================================
Internal draft: align website, FAQ, intake, and contracts before launch.
All amounts are CAD unless noted otherwise.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FEES (AUTHORITATIVE FOR THIS DOCUMENT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Basic:   CAD 220 one-time setup + CAD 25 per month (ongoing)
  Mid:     CAD 350 one-time setup + CAD 25 per month (ongoing, no intro discount)
  Premium: CAD 400 one-time setup + CAD 18 per month for the first two months,
           then CAD 25 per month (ongoing). Disclose the step-up clearly on
           pricing pages and at signup.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
APPLICATIONS (ALL TIERS — SAME COMMITMENT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Every tier: at least 20 quality applications per week (subject to market
  fit — you do not apply to irrelevant roles only to hit a number). Volume
  is not what separates the tiers; documents, coaching, and outreach do.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ALL TIERS — OPERATIONS YOU PROVIDE REGARDLESS OF PACKAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Weekly email summary of activity (roles targeted, submissions, anything
  that needs the client’s input). Reasonable response SLA (e.g. reply within
  24–48 business hours) unless you publish a faster Premium SLA.

================================================================================
TIER 1 — BASIC
================================================================================
POSITIONING
  Entry tier: resume and cover letter polish, then consistent applications.
  Clear limits reduce disputes and protect your time.

ONE-TIME SETUP
  CAD 220

MONTHLY
  CAD 25

WHAT IS INCLUDED
  Resume and cover letters
    - Up to 3 resume variants (up to 3 target role families or keyword sets).
    - Up to 3 cover letter variants aligned to those tracks.
    - Define “variant” in client-facing terms (e.g. repositioned headline,
      summary, and priority bullets vs full ground-up rewrite) and cap
      revision rounds (e.g. 2 rounds per variant within 14–30 days of
      delivery).

  Applications
    - 20+ quality applications per week, as defined in “Applications (all
      tiers)” above.
    - Discovery via job boards (e.g. LinkedIn, Indeed) with submission
      mainly through employer career sites where practical. State whether
      LinkedIn Easy Apply is in or out of scope for Basic.

  Not included in Basic (reserve for higher tiers unless you add them here)
    - LinkedIn profile rewrite beyond what is needed for applications.
    - Mock interviews and structured interview coaching.
    - Offer negotiation support.
    - Cold email to recruiters or hiring managers on the client’s behalf.
    - “Unlimited” rewrites or unlimited role pivots.

================================================================================
TIER 2 — MID
================================================================================
POSITIONING
  Step up from Basic: same application engine, plus profile polish, one mock
  interview, and interview-prep materials. Intended to sit clearly between
  Basic and Premium on value (pricing psychology: anchor — do not use
  “lure” in customer-facing copy; say “balanced upgrade” or similar).

ONE-TIME SETUP
  CAD 350

MONTHLY
  CAD 25 (no promotional discount on the monthly fee)

WHAT IS INCLUDED
  Everything in Basic, plus:
    - LinkedIn profile cleanup (headline, About, and keyword alignment —
      scope this so it is not open-ended).
    - Interview prep: 1 mock interview (state length, e.g. 30–45 minutes,
      and format: live video vs phone).
    - Training materials to support interview prep (bound this: e.g. one
      curated resource list plus short guidance, or up to 60 minutes of
      curated content per month — pick what you can sustain).

  Not included in Mid (keep for Premium unless you explicitly add here)
    - Multiple mock interviews per month beyond the single session above.
    - Offer negotiation.
    - Ongoing cold email to recruiters and hiring managers at scale.

================================================================================
TIER 3 — PREMIUM
================================================================================
POSITIONING
  Full package: more document variants, deeper interview support, offer
  support, and capped outbound outreach — still with written limits so
  scope stays deliverable.

ONE-TIME SETUP
  CAD 400

MONTHLY
  CAD 18 per month for the first two months, then CAD 25 per month. State
  this everywhere clients see price (cards, FAQ, checkout, welcome email).

WHAT IS INCLUDED
  Everything in Mid, plus:
    - Up to 5 resume variants and up to 5 matching cover letter variants
      (same “variant” definition and revision caps as Basic, scaled to five
      tracks — publish the cap in plain language).
    - Interview prep: 3 mock interviews (define length and period, e.g.
      3× 45 minutes per month or per rolling 30 days — choose one rule).
    - Offer negotiation support (not legal advice): e.g. one active offer
      review per month with written talking points — tighten to what you
      will actually do.
    - Cold email to recruiters and hiring managers: add a monthly cap
      (e.g. 10–25 messages), client approval on templates, and rules
      against misrepresentation.

  Optional differentiators (add only if you will commit)
    - Faster SLA than Basic/Mid for urgent interview deadlines.
    - Curated learning plan beyond Mid’s materials (time cap per month).

  Still excluded (unless you change strategy)
    - Guarantees of interviews or employment (keep intake disclaimer).

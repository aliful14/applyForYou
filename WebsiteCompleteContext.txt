1. BRAND & BUSINESS (HIGH LEVEL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Trade / marketing name: ApplyForYou

What the service does:
  - Done-for-you job applications for people in Canada (primary audience:
    general Canadian job seekers, including newcomers, grads, career switchers).
  - Resume polish (ATS-oriented), tailored cover letters, weekly applications
    across LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, and company career sites.
  - Client focuses on interviews and prep; the operator + team handle the
    application grind.

Pricing reflected on the website (as of site build):
  - $200 CAD one-time: resume polish + multiple cover letters + LinkedIn quick
    review.
  - $75 CAD / month while actively applying (cancel anytime).
  - Explicit on site: no job guarantee — activity-based promise only.

Payment on site:
  - None. Intake via Tally; payment via Interac e-Transfer after personal
    email and agreement.

Operator model:
  - Sole proprietorship framing; optional Bangladesh contractor for manual
    steps (mentioned in broader business plan, not required on landing page).

8. RUNNING THE SITE LOCALLY (WINDOWS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  cd "c:\Drive\git repo\JobApplyProject\website"
  .\serve.ps1

  The script tries ports 5500 through 5509 and prints the working URL, e.g.
  http://localhost:5501/

  Force a port:
    .\serve.ps1 -Port 8080

  Stop: Ctrl+C in that terminal window.

  If execution policy blocks scripts (first time):
    Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope CurrentUser RemoteSigned

  Python / Node were not reliably available on the user's machine; serve.ps1
  uses System.Net.HttpListener and does not require Python.
